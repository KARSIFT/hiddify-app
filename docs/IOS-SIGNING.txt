Hiddify Karsift iOS build — signing required

This IPA is unsigned and cannot be installed directly on an iPhone.
It includes the app and its HiddifyPacketTunnel VPN extension.

When an Apple Developer membership and signing identities are available:
1. Register com.karsift.hiddify and com.karsift.hiddify.HiddifyPacketTunnel.
2. Register the shared app group group.com.karsift.hiddify for both targets.
3. Enable the Network Extensions / packet-tunnel capability and generate
   matching provisioning profiles for the app and extension. Match every
   entitlement to the capabilities permitted by the signing profiles.
4. Open ios/Runner.xcworkspace in Xcode, select your development team for
   both targets and archive the same source revision shown in BUILD-INFO-iOS.txt.
5. Export with your chosen distribution method and its provisioning profiles.
   For development or ad-hoc distribution, register the destination devices.

Alternatively, a signing pipeline can sign the nested frameworks, extension
and app in that order, embed each target's matching provisioning profile,
and repackage Payload as an IPA. A normal personal/free signing profile is
not sufficient for the VPN extension's required capabilities.

No Apple certificates, private keys or provisioning profiles are included.
